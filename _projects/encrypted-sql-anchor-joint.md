This project studies plaintext recovery attacks against encrypted SQL systems supporting multi-attribute boolean and join queries. The attack uses high-confidence anchors and optimal transport to incrementally recover ciphertext-plaintext mappings under joint distribution leakage.

The project investigates how encrypted relational query systems leak statistical information through equality patterns and joint distributions. By combining optimal transport-based matching with anchor-guided iterative inference, the attack improves recovery accuracy and robustness over prior methods.
